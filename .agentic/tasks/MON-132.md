# MON-132 - Cut over payable and procurement UI money

## Objective and implementation scope

Bill, debit note, purchase order, requisition, goods receipt and recurring bill lists/details/editors plus their shared create-drawer sections and line-items-editor integration. Preserve documented fixed-two compatibility and physical quantity contracts; exact aliases and previews.

## Inputs

Parent [MON-129](MON-129.md); MONEY_MANIFEST and MONEY_BOUNDARIES.json; canonical exact adapters and adopted domain wire inventories; [source](../sources/SOURCE.md#money-and-database-architecture). DEC-005 permits behavioral/layout review without screenshots.

- [ ] All consumers in this slice have explicit manifest ownership and use exact money input, arithmetic, presentation and adopted aliases.
- [ ] Meaningful precision, malformed/range, compatibility, currency-scale and preview/saved amount checks pass; English behavior is preserved.
- [ ] Monetary float/fixed-two assumptions are removed or documented as explicit compatibility exceptions; parent integration stays open.
