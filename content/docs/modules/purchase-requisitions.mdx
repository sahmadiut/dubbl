title: Purchase Requisitions
description: Request and approve purchases before creating purchase orders.

Purchase requisitions record internal requests to buy before creating a purchase
order. Navigate to **Purchases > Requisitions**.

## Creating and submitting a requisition

Create a requisition with descriptions, physical quantities and estimated prices,
a request date, and optional notes/reference/required date. A supplier is optional
until conversion. Requisitions calculate no tax, even when a tax reference is saved.
Click **Submit for Approval** on the draft to change its server status to submitted.

Submitted requests can be approved or rejected by users with purchase approval
permission. Rejection records an optional reason. This requisition workflow does
not automatically route approval steps or send notifications.

## Converting to a purchase order

An approved requisition with a supplier can be converted once to a draft purchase
order. Its saved amounts, lines and currency are copied; the order date is today
in UTC and its delivery date is the requisition's required date. The requisition
becomes converted and links to the order. Approved/converted requisitions cannot
be edited or deleted. Draft deletion retains historical lines.

Writes check tenant references and locked request dates. Conversion also checks
the new purchase-order date. Draft supplier/date/reference/notes edits and exact
price aliases are available through the [API Reference](/docs/api-reference) and
[MCP tools](/docs/guides/mcp).
