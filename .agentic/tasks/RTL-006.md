## Objective and implementation scope

Preserve exact caller strings, selection, copy callbacks, field identifiers, native validation and explicit direction overrides. This foundation does not claim module-wide adoption, font packaging or rendered-width/output qualification.

- Parent [RTL-002](RTL-002.md), [requirements](../sources/SOURCE.md#rtl-uiux-strategy) and prior RTL-001 evidence.
