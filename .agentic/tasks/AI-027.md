- [ ] Keep reading position during streaming, provide Jump to latest, milestone announcements and accessible dialogs. Handle long/truncated results, provider/quota/permission errors and sanitized model links/content without leaking raw diagnostics or private reasoning.
- [ ] Behavior tests use real composed UI plus deterministic persisted-run fixtures for dependent tools, clarification, batch rejection/staleness, cancellation, offline/reload and permission loss. Verify exact outcomes and accessible interactions in English/Persian at the specified layouts; screenshots are optional.
