# MON-016 ? Adopt exact contracts at public and opaque boundaries

## Objective and implementation scope

Public payment/portal/signing routes, provider/webhook payloads, backups/restore, imports/exports, embedded JSON and remaining direct NextResponse/JSON forwarding not owned by other children. Preserve external provider limits/token scope/immutable payload contracts; adopt explicit representations and guard before writes. Complete cross-domain boundary inventory and regression coverage, splitting if needed.

## Inputs

Parent [MON-012](MON-012.md), MON-011 evidence, ADR-006, [migration](../sources/SOURCE.md#database-and-currency-migration) and [API compatibility](../sources/SOURCE.md#api-backward-compatibility), MONEY_MANIFEST and actual source.

- [ ] Every boundary in the adopted slice has documented inputs, outputs, units, exact aliases and supported business ranges.
