## Objective and implementation scope

reports/payment-performance and corresponding MCP parity. Preserve payment-timing and jurisdiction-neutral semantics; exact single-currency money sums and supported aggregate units.

## Inputs

## Acceptance criteria

currency selection and visible report errors. Actual migrated PostgreSQL fixtures,

money inventory/gates and diff checks passed. See evidence/MON-113-attempt-1.md
and evidence/MON-113-review-1.md. No schema/migration changes or blocker.

independent accounting and full-int64 qualification are not claimed.
