The Payroll module handles employee and contractor compensation. Navigate to **Payroll** in the sidebar.

## Employees

Register employees with their compensation details. Navigate to **Payroll > Employees**.

### Employee Fields

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| Name | Employee name |
| Email | Contact email |
| Employee Number | Unique identifier |
| Position | Job title |
| Annual Salary | Yearly compensation (cents) |
| Pay Frequency | Weekly, Biweekly, or Monthly |
| Tax Rate | Tax rate in basis points (2200 = 22%) |
| Start Date | Employment start date |

Employee and contractor master APIs accept legacy numeric cents and additive
`salaryMinor` / `hourlyRateMinor` integer strings. Salary is annual; hourly rate
is per hour. Aliases must agree and support values up to 9007199254740991 cents.
Hourly rates can be cleared with null. Tax rate remains basis points, dates are
Gregorian, and stored amounts never rescale when a currency is selected. Currency
changes require no existing pay items or contractor payments. Editing a master
record preserves historical payroll and payment amounts.

## Contractors

Manage contractors separately from employees. Navigate to **Payroll > Contractors**. Contractors have different tax treatment and are typically paid per invoice rather than through payroll runs.

## Payroll Runs

### Creating a Run

1. Navigate to **Payroll > Runs > New Payroll Run**
2. Set the pay period start and end dates
3. The system auto-generates payroll items for all active employees
4. Gross pay is calculated from annual salary divided by pay frequency

### Processing

Click **Process Payroll** to finalize the run:

1. Creates a journal entry:
   - **Debit** Wages & Salaries (5100) - total gross
   - **Credit** Tax Payable (2200) - total tax
   - **Credit** Bank (1100) - total net pay
2. Marks the run as completed

### Pay Calculations

```
Gross = Annual Salary / Pay Periods per Year
Tax = Gross x (Tax Rate / 10000)
Net = Gross - Tax - Deductions
```

Pay periods per year:
- Monthly = 12
- Biweekly = 26
- Weekly = 52

### Payslips

After processing, view and download individual payslips at **Payroll > Runs > [Run] > Payslips**.

## Timesheets

Track employee hours worked. Navigate to **Payroll > Timesheets**.

- Create timesheets for pay periods
- Employees log hours by day
- Approved timesheets feed into payroll calculations
- Link timesheet entries to projects for billable hour tracking

## Leave Management

Navigate to **Payroll > Leave** to manage time off.

- **Leave requests** - Employees submit requests for time off
- **Leave balances** - Track accrued and used leave days
- **Leave types** - Annual leave, sick leave, personal leave, etc.
- **Approval** - Managers approve or decline requests

## Tax Forms

Navigate to **Payroll > Tax Forms** for year-end and periodic tax documents.

- Generate tax forms for employees (e.g., W-2, P60)
- View and download completed forms
- Track filing status

## Compensation

Navigate to **Payroll > Compensation** to manage salary structures and conduct compensation reviews.

## Analytics and Forecasting

- **Payroll Analytics** (**Payroll > Analytics**) - Visualize payroll costs, headcount trends, and department breakdowns
- **Payroll Forecasting** (**Payroll > Forecasting**) - Project future payroll costs based on current employees and planned changes

## Payroll Reports

Navigate to **Payroll > Reports** for payroll-specific reports including:

- Payroll summary by period
- Tax withholding summary
- Department cost breakdown
- Year-to-date totals

## Settings

Configure payroll defaults at **Payroll > Settings**, including default tax rates, pay frequencies, and leave policies.

## Employee Self-Service

Navigate to **Payroll > Self-Service** for the employee portal where team members can view their payslips, submit leave requests, and update personal details.

## Configuration API money contracts

Payroll settings, deduction types and employee deductions, employee tax elections, tax brackets and tax allowances retain integer cents fields and add matching `Minor` strings (for example `defaultAmountMinor`, `additionalWithholdingMinor` and `ssWageBaseCentsMinor`). Numeric and string inputs must agree, with a supported range of 0 through 9007199254740991. Nullable fields can be cleared with null; omitted update fields retain their values. Tax thresholds/brackets/allowances are annual cents; fixed deductions and extra withholding are per pay period.

Rates ending in `Bp`, default tax rate and bracket rate are integer basis points (100 = 1%). Deduction percentages use decimal percent; existing PostgreSQL real controls require values exactly representable in binary32, such as 2.5. Unsupported percentages such as 2.9 reject instead of silently rounding. Allowance counts remain nonnegative int32. Employee elections use `additionalWithholding`, a single extra per-period amount, rather than separate federal/state fields.

The allowance configuration API is available at `/api/v1/payroll/tax/allowances` with list/create and individual get/edit/delete operations. It stores annual allowance/standard deduction settings, requiring `manage:tax-config`, and rejects duplicate live jurisdiction/year configurations. Configuration editing does not certify jurisdiction-specific payroll calculations or current statutory tax rules.

## Time, leave and shift API contracts

Timesheet and leave amounts stay **hours**, and shift premiums stay decimal **percent** (25 means +25%, not 2500 basis points). Existing storage accepts values that save unchanged, such as 7.5 or 0.25. Unsupported values such as 0.1 reject with an error instead of silently rounding. No `Minor` alias applies to physical hours or premiums.

Timesheet entries can be added or deleted only while the sheet is draft, and their Gregorian dates must fall within its period. Approval/rejection requires a submitted sheet. Leave requests use ordered Gregorian dates within one year; split requests at the year boundary. Approval requires a single sufficient leave balance for that request year and updates it atomically. Approved leave cannot be changed through the generic request editor. Self-service resolves your employee profile from organization membership.
