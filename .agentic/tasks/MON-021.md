# MON-021 - Adopt exact payment expense and banking contracts

## Objective and implementation scope

Payments/allocations/batches/scheduled payments, expenses and claims, banking accounts/transactions/transfers/reconciliation/import/rules and corresponding REST/MCP and bulk operations. Inspect persisted and opaque rule monetary inputs; coordinate opaque serializer coverage with MON-016 and import qualification with DATA-001.

## Inputs

Parent [MON-014](MON-014.md), MON-011 evidence, ADR-006, MONEY_MANIFEST and actual source. Requirements: [migration](../sources/SOURCE.md#database-and-currency-migration) and [API compatibility](../sources/SOURCE.md#api-backward-compatibility).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every boundary in this slice has documented inputs, outputs, units, exact aliases and supported ranges.
- [ ] Actual REST/MCP fixtures cover legacy and exact clients, authorization and organization isolation.
- [ ] Invalid or unsupported inputs fail before mutation; responses preserve numeric compatibility without bigint crashes or silent precision/unit loss.
