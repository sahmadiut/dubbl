# MON-078 - Adopt exact inventory assembly contracts

## Objective and implementation scope

BOM/component and assembly order CRUD/completion REST/MCP; exact component/finished costs and transactional balanced valuation with physical units preserved.

## Inputs

Parent [MON-024](MON-024.md), MON-011 evidence, ADR-006, MONEY_MANIFEST and actual source. Requirements: [migration](../sources/SOURCE.md#database-and-currency-migration) and [API compatibility](../sources/SOURCE.md#api-backward-compatibility). Preserve MON-052 goods-receipt writer behavior.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every boundary in this slice has documented inputs, outputs, units, exact aliases and supported ranges.
- [ ] Actual REST/MCP fixtures cover legacy and exact clients, authorization and organization isolation.
- [ ] Invalid or unsupported inputs fail before mutation; responses preserve numeric compatibility without bigint crashes or silent precision/unit loss.

## Verification

Meaningful operation fixtures, typecheck and lint. No builds or unrequested dev server.

## Handoff

MON-077 adds nullable FIFO remainingValue/consumption value with unchanged
historical unitCost. Reuse shared inventory valuation engine and
INVENTORY_VALUATION_WIRE_CONTRACTS.md. Actual FIFO carrying values may not equal
remainingQuantity times unitCost after landed capitalization; assembly preflights
and reversals must use exact carrying/consumed values. MON-077 does not qualify
assembly writers or remove procurement receipt divisibility restrictions.
