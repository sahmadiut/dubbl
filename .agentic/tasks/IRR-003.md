## Objective and implementation scope

operations and critical IRR invoice/payment entry. Include existing organization
history, not only synthetic organizations preseeded with defaultCurrency=IRR.
Prepare repeatable flag-on candidate tests and flag-off rejection tests without
deploying the candidate or treating test harness overrides as runtime permission.

under DEC-008, integer minor units, canonical money contracts and existing data.
