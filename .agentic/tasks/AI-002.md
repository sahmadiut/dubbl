## Objective and implementation scope

Harden and qualify identity resolution across session, API-key and MCP access, including custom roles and advisors.

This task implements the owner-requested AI feature; it does not authorize deployment or relax existing financial/release gates.

## Inputs

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Removed members and revoked or expired credentials fail closed; MCP custom-role permissions are resolved from current scoped membership without stale-role fallback.

- [ ] Negative REST/MCP tests cover custom roles, advisor limitations, cross-organization IDs, revocation during a run, forged organization arguments and site-admin versus organization-admin separation.
