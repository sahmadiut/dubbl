## Bills

Bills track what you owe to suppliers.

### Bill Lifecycle

```
Draft → Received → Partial/Paid
                 → Overdue
                 → Void
```

When a bill is **received**, a journal entry is created:
- **Debit** Expense accounts (per line item)
- **Credit** Accounts Payable

Bills submitted for approval first enter **Pending Approval**. Final approval
posts the same ledger and stock effects as receiving a draft. Rejection returns
the bill to draft. Approval requests with several steps post only after the last
assigned approver acts.

Receiving or approving records expense/inventory, recoverable VAT and Accounts
Payable in one transaction with stock, receipt matching and audit. Goods already
received through a goods receipt are not received again. Voiding an unsettled bill
reverses saved journal amounts, FX and original stock value. Recorded payments,
locked periods, consumed FIFO receipts and unsupported historical records must
be resolved before voiding.

### Recording Payments

Click **Record Payment** on a bill to:
- Create a journal entry (DR Accounts Payable, CR Bank)
- Update the bill's paid amount and status

## Purchase Orders

Purchase orders formalize purchases before they become bills.

### PO Lifecycle

```
Draft → Sent → Partial/Received → Closed
                                 → Void
```

### Converting to Bills

Accepted purchase orders can be **converted to bills**, copying all line items. The PO status updates to "closed" after conversion.

## Bill Numbering

Bills use the prefix `BILL-` and purchase orders use `PO-`, both auto-numbered sequentially.

## Supplier debit notes

Supplier debit notes record returns or overcharges as AP credits. Drafts can be
edited or deleted. Sending posts the credit; applying it reduces a recognized
bill's outstanding balance for the same supplier and currency. Voiding reverses
the saved accounting and unwinds applications together. Void active linked notes
before voiding their original bill.

Complete matching stock returns use original bill receipt costs and unconsumed
average/FIFO stock. Partial stock, GRNI, tracked stock and specialized expense tax
need further qualification and currently reject. Allocation also requires matching
saved carrying FX; differing FX is separately qualified. Email delivery follows
accounting recognition and can be retried separately if it fails.

See [API Reference](/docs/api-reference) for exact price/amount aliases, retained
REST major-unit and MCP minor-unit price conventions, and supported safe ranges.

## PDF Download

Bills and purchase orders can be downloaded as PDFs. The layout uses your configured template with your branding, accent color, and notes. To customize, go to **Settings > Templates**. See [Templates](/docs/settings/document-templates) for all available options.
