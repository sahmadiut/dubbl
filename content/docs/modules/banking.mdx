## Bank Accounts

Create bank accounts linked to your chart of accounts. Each account owns its own statement imports, transactions, and reconciliation workflow.

### Fields

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Account Name | Display name (e.g., "Business Checking") |
| Account Number | Masked number for display |
| Bank Name | Name of the bank |
| Currency | Account currency |
| Country | Country or region of the account |
| Account Type | Checking, savings, credit card, cash, loan, investment, or other |
| Accent Color | Visual identity used across the banking UI |
| GL Account | Chart of accounts link |

## Importing Transactions

Import bank statements into a specific bank account:

1. Navigate to your bank account
2. Click **Import Statement**
3. Upload a file or paste raw statement data
4. Preview detected rows, duplicates, warnings, and date ranges
5. Confirm the import

Supported formats:
- CSV
- TSV
- QIF
- OFX / QFX / QBO
- CAMT.052 / CAMT.053 / CAMT.054
- MT940 / MT942
- BAI2

Every imported transaction stores provenance such as the import batch and source format.

## Reconciliation

Reconciliation matches bank transactions to your accounting records.

### Transaction Statuses

- **Unreconciled** — Not yet matched
- **Reconciled** — Matched to a journal entry
- **Excluded** — Intentionally ignored (e.g., transfers between own accounts)

### Reconciliation Workflow

1. Navigate to the bank account → **Reconcile**
2. Create a new reconciliation session with date range
3. Review unreconciled transactions
4. Match each transaction to invoices, bills, or journal entries
5. Mark as reconciled or excluded

The fuzzy matcher suggests matches based on amount, date, reference, and description.

### Categorizing bank movements

Code a movement to a ledger category, or split it across categories whose amounts
sum exactly to the bank amount. Tax and transaction-date FX are included in the
balanced journal. Locked periods and excluded movements cannot be coded.
Correcting a plain categorization replaces its journal while retaining saved FX.
Bulk coding reports each success or failure separately.

Recording an outgoing movement as an expense creates an already-paid claim linked
to its journal. Its currency and item total must match the bank movement. It does
not need another approval or reimbursement. Bank-created expenses cannot be
recategorized or recreated through these actions; resolving their claim and bank
reversal together remains a separate workflow.

### Matching invoices, bills and existing cash

Match the entire bank movement to one invoice/bill, or split it across outstanding
documents of the same contact and currency. Incoming money settles invoices;
outgoing money settles bills. Allocations must cover the full movement and cannot
exceed outstanding balances. Partial coverage and mixed contacts/currencies are
rejected. The payment, saved carrying and cash FX journal, document balances,
bank links and audit commit together; the statement balance remains unchanged.

An existing posted cash payment can be linked without recording it again when
its bank, currency, amount and direction agree exactly. Credit-note, debit-note
and prepayment application records are noncash and cannot substitute for a bank
cash movement. Qualified base-currency journal entries can also be matched by
their exact bank ledger amount. Matching retains saved FX and respects locked
periods. Undo and statement-session reconciliation remain separate workflows.

### Transfers between your accounts

Match an unreconciled movement as a transfer to another active own bank account
in the same currency. Select an equal, opposite statement line in the other
account, or let the app create a mirror. One journal debits the receiving bank
and credits the sending bank; both movements become linked and reconciled.
Both statement dates must be open. An already linked or excluded line cannot be
matched again. Cross-currency cash transfers are unsupported.

Transfers keep saved statement and running balances unchanged. New movements
contribute to transaction totals and the ledger. Standalone API/MCP transfer
creation also records a linked pair; each call creates a new transfer, so inspect
the records before retrying after an uncertain result. Undo remains a separate
workflow.

### Statement checks and undo

Start a statement check with its Gregorian dates and opening/closing balances in
the bank's currency. Balance inputs and displays respect that currency's decimal
scale. Every nonignored movement must be accounted for before completion, and
both the statement movements and ledger balance must agree with the closing
balance. Overlapping statement windows are rejected. Checks and write-offs for
foreign-currency balances require separate currency qualification; the report
shows the statement and base-currency books separately.

Undoing a check reopens affected completed statements. Existing matched cash
payments and journals retain their accounting. Payments created from statement
matching restore invoice/bill balances; bank-created expense/category/transfer
postings receive exact reversals using their saved amounts and exchange rates.
Both transfer legs unwind together, with generated mirrors removed and original
statement dates/amounts/balances retained. Expense undo also requires expense
management permission. Locked or ambiguous history must be resolved before undo.
