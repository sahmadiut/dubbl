# MON-133 - Cut over core cash tax and account UI money

## Objective and implementation scope

Payments/allocations, expenses/mileage, banking/reconciliation, tax settlement/settings, account ledger/detail, opening balances and remaining core list/detail/shared create-drawer consumers. Enumerate from MONEY_BOUNDARIES.json; preserve compatibility contracts and nonmoney units. Auxiliary reports/jobs/public/provider consumers stay MON-008.

## Inputs

Parent [MON-129](MON-129.md); MONEY_MANIFEST and MONEY_BOUNDARIES.json; canonical exact adapters and adopted domain wire inventories; [source](../sources/SOURCE.md#money-and-database-architecture). DEC-005 permits behavioral/layout review without screenshots.
