Compatibility remains the existing safe numeric business range. USD 1250 stays
1250, JPY/IRR 1250 stays 1250 and KWD 1250 stays 1250; presentation follows saved
currency scale. Signed values remain supported where the owning operation allows
them. Alias disagreement, hidden malformed money, unknown currency, mixed
statement units, unsafe derived display sums and malformed success envelopes
reject. No schema/migration, new API/MCP operation or production flag change.

The regression suite is local behavioral/static and disposable PostgreSQL
coverage. It does not claim live browser layout, provider delivery, production
IRR, native-language review or hosted CI qualification.
